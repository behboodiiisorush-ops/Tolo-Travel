# Driving quiz — idea pool for future episodes

Pick from here (or invent new ones); when a topic is used, move it to USED_QUESTIONS.md.
General rules that hold in most countries — nothing tied to one country's law, signs with text,
flags or emblems. Each needs a visual the template can draw (add a new `visual` kind if needed).

- blue circle sign = mandatory direction (باید این سمت بری)
- red-border triangle = warning / danger ahead
- yield sign (inverted triangle) = حق تقدم بده
- solid white line in the middle = سبقت ممنوع / no crossing
- broken white line = you may change lanes / overtake when safe
- zebra crossing with a pedestrian waiting = بایست و راه بده
- roundabout: who has priority (cars already inside)
- school bus with flashing lights stopped = stop
- green arrow + red light = only that direction may go
- hazard lights: when to use them (stopped / danger), not for parking anywhere
- fog: low beam, not high beam
- aquaplaning: ease off the gas, don't brake hard, hold the wheel straight
- seat belt in the back seat too
- phone while driving: only hands-free / not at all
- tyre tread / tyre pressure check when cold
- children: child seat, never in front of an airbag
- tired driver: stop and rest (coffee isn't enough)
- night: dazzle from oncoming car → look at the right edge of the road
- railway crossing with flashing red lights → stop
- parking on a hill: turn wheels toward the curb (downhill)
- blind spot: shoulder check before changing lane
- motorcycle ahead: keep more distance
- ice / black ice: gentle steering, no sudden braking
- reversing: look back, not only mirrors
- emergency triangle: place it well behind the car
- engine temperature warning light
- ABS: press the brake hard and keep it pressed
- speed bump sign / uneven road warning
- end of all restrictions sign (white circle with black diagonal stripes)
- headlight flash from oncoming car: something ahead, slow down
