# «پناهگاه ۴ — چراغ‌های جاده» — story + lines for the user to record

Starts exactly where episode 3 ends: the bus is stuck in the forest at night, dogs around it, the beast walking
up the road, the car's headlights coming from the front. Vertical POV through «من» (and a 16:9 long version).

1. Cold open: the beast is ten steps from the bus; the car blasts its horn, a roof floodlight blazes on, the beast
   screams and flees into the trees, the dogs after it -> they fear strong light.
2. Two figures in white suits with glass-visor masks step out (the radio voice): they come from «پناهگاه بزرگ»,
   room for three only.
3. Argument on the bus: the rescuer says don't trust them, the old man has seen them before, Mom wants to send Lena.
4. Discovery: on the radio I overhear them talk about «نمونه‌ها», not people; in a cage in the back of their car an
   eye glows — the same kind of creature as the one from the wall.
5. End: Lena takes a stranger's hand and is getting into the car; I shout; the floodlight goes out; glowing eyes
   all round the forest. Final question card.

Speakers: Me (narrator + me), Lena, Mom, Rescuer (driver), Old man, Stranger 1 (calm, leader), Stranger 2.
Record each line as its own file named by its key (n1.mp3, me1.mp3, ...), or one take in this order.
