## 2. Logline
A frightened 19-year-old crosses a dead city to rescue his sister from the research centre that holds her —
and learns, one door at a time, that the outbreak did not reach the city before him. It came with him.

## 3. Premise in one paragraph
Arin gets a broken video message from his sister Nika: she is alive inside "the Centre", a sealed research
facility under a hill, and something is wrong with her. A stranger phones and tells him to get there before
dawn. Inside, Dr. Elena runs a "safe shelter" for a few survivors under strange rules. Behind the red door,
Arin finds a child's room labelled SUBJECT 001 with his own drawings in it. A power failure opens every cell
and the Centre falls. Arin finds Nika — Subject 002, slowly turning — and learns the real purpose of the
experiment: to make a human carrier who never shows symptoms and passes every scanner. At the quarantine gate
at dawn, Elena's last recording confirms it: Arin is Patient Zero of the outbreak outside. The scanner says
he is CLEAR. He is the one thing the gate can never stop.

## 4. Story canon — never change
- **Arin (آرین)** — 19, male protagonist, **Subject 001**, Patient Zero of the outbreak *outside* the facility.
  He did **not** create the virus. Black centre-parted hair, dark eyes, small scar near the (left) eyebrow,
  dark practical jacket, dark trousers. Frightened at first, intelligent, determined.
- **Nika (نیکا)** — Arin's sister, **Subject 002**, held inside the facility. Her infection progresses through
  the film. She still recognises Arin for part of the story (up to her last scene, 7.4).
- **Dr. Elena (دکتر النا)** — scientist in charge of the experiments. Knows Arin's history, hides it.
  Morally complicated: she caused the first leak, she wants to cure Nika, and she sold the "carrier" result to
  pay for that cure.
- **Sara (سارا)** — survivor who joins Arin. Suspicious of the facility from the first minute. Becomes attached
  to Arin's mission. Consistent throughout.
- **Mark (مارک)** — security guard. Knows more than he says. Twelve years ago he was the guard who opened the
  door for the child Arin. He is the mysterious caller.
- **The virus** was developed and studied by the facility. Arin is not its creator.
- Supporting (not in the canon list, defined here): **Sam (سام)** — survivor, ~32, delivery driver, talkative,
  frightened, causes the power failure. **Kaveh (کاوه)** — former researcher, infected but lucid in cell B3-09,
  gives the warning in Ch3. Soldiers at the gate (no insignia). Infected people (see §8).

## 5. Hidden backstory (the truth the film uncovers — audience learns it in this order, see §6)
| when | what really happened |
|---|---|
| ~20 years ago | Elena's lab at the Centre develops **NX** — a regenerative virus meant to restart dead tissue. It restarts the body and erases the person. |
| 12 years ago | The **first leak** — Elena's mistake. Arin and Nika's parents (technicians at the Centre) die sealing it. Arin (7) and Nika (4) are infected. Arin shows no symptoms at all: the virus lives in him, harmless to him, permanent. Nika resists only partly; she is kept "asleep" most of the years. Arin is held for a year as **SUBJECT 001** (the restraint injury leaves the eyebrow scar). |
| 11 years ago | Mark, a young guard, opens a door and walks the boy out. Arin is sedated, told a car accident killed his parents and that Nika died in hospital. He grows up in the city with a foster aunt (dead before the film). He remembers almost nothing — one image: holding a small hand in a white corridor, a hummed lullaby. |
| years since | Elena's funding comes from an unnamed **client**. The client is not interested in a cure; it wants Arin's trait: a carrier who passes every scanner. Elena agrees, to keep the lab — and Nika — alive. |
| 26 days ago | "Free health check" at a city clinic: Arin receives a **booster** (NX activator). From now on he is contagious through blood and saliva, still with no symptoms. This is the **field test**. |
| 24–3 days ago | First cases in the city: his flatmate, then the driver of the night bus he takes every day, then the clinic. The city falls in three weeks. The Centre seals itself. |
| the night of the film | The field test is over. Mark, ashamed, records Nika and secretly calls Arin. Elena already knew Arin would come — the client wants him "collected" at dawn. |

## 7. Themes and tone
- Guilt that is not your fault — and what you owe anyway. Arin is innocent and still the cause.
- Being used as a tool by people who say they love you (Elena with Nika; the client with Arin).
- Tone: grounded, quiet, adult. Fear comes from sound, darkness, waiting and what people hide — not from gore.
  Violence is shown briefly and mostly off-frame; blood is used as a story element (the cut palm motif).

## 10. Project-rule compliance (Tolo Travel rules)
- No hijab on anyone (Elena, Sara, Nika wear their hair visible; Nika's is loose).
- No flags, emblems, logos, unit patches, place names or real-world signage. Soldiers: plain uniforms, masks.
  Facility signage: neutral codes only (B1, B3-09, SUBJECT 001). The client is never named.
- The story never depends on the audience. It ends with one question to the viewer (card after the title).
- Voices: the user records them in ElevenLabs. Every line in the screenplay carries its delivery as English
  v3 audio tags in square brackets. A silent version (no sound) is delivered; lip movement (`talking=True`)
  is kept on every line.
- All times given to the user in Berlin time.

## 11. Production analysis — problems to solve before PHASE 3 (reported honestly)
1. **Render time (biggest risk).** 86,400 Cycles frames at 1080p with humans, hair, glass and volumetrics.
   On this cloud container (CPU only, no GPU) a realistic Cycles frame is ~3–10 min → **6 months to 1.5 years**
   of continuous rendering, in a container that is reclaimed when idle. Options:
   a) Cycles on a GPU render farm or the user's own GPU machine (~20–60 s/frame → 3–8 weeks);
   b) EEVEE (what our recent films use) for most shots, Cycles only for hero shots;
   c) Cycles at low samples + denoiser + 12 fps holds for still moments. **Decision needed from the user.**
2. **Characters.** The canon defines a new cast (Arin 19, Nika, Elena, Sara, Mark, Sam). Our permanent cast
   (`blender/cast/`) has a different آرین (15, joker). These people will be added as new keys in `cast.py` with
   their own sheets (e.g. `zc_arin`), built on the same MPFB/MakeHuman pipeline — not redesigned per scene.
3. **Rigs.** MPFB rigs give a full skeleton, face shape keys, blinking, lip flap. True IK/FK control rigs,
   finger rigs and cloth/hair simulation for 60 minutes are far beyond what a script-driven pipeline can do
   reliably; plan: Mixamo clips (`blender/mocap/`) + procedural layers + IK foot locking, cloth sim only on
   hero shots. New clips (crying, arguing, falling, dying, grabbing, crawling) must be downloaded by the user
   from Mixamo.
4. **Blender MCP tools** are not connected in this session. Everything runs as `bpy` scripts through
   `blender/cast/bpy.sh`, with renders inspected after each step.
5. **Voices.** ~300 dialogue lines for the user to record; voice scripts will be split per chapter.
6. **Scale of writing.** The screenplay is timed scene by scene to 60:00; exact shot timing is set in
   PHASE 7 (shot list) against the recorded voices.
