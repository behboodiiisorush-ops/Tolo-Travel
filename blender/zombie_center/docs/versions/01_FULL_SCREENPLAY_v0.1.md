# CHAPTER 1 — THE LAST MESSAGE · آخرین پیام
**00:00 – 07:30 · 22:40–23:55 · city, mine road, facility entrance**
Objective: get Arin from "alone in a dead city" to "a gun in his face at the Centre's door".

### 1.1 — EXT. AVENUE, ABANDONED CITY — NIGHT · 00:00–00:50
FIRST FRAME: extreme close-up. A phone lies face-down on wet asphalt, buzzing, its light leaking from under
the edge into a puddle. In the puddle's reflection, far behind, a figure stands in the middle of the road,
swaying. (Small line, bottom left, 2 s: AGETOBUDI presents.)

[3P, low] A night avenue: dead traffic lights blinking amber, cars left with doors open, a city bus on its
side across two lanes. Thin fog, wet reflections. A hand comes out from under the bus — slowly — and pulls the
phone in. Under the bus: **ARIN** (19), breathing hard through his nose, holding his own mouth shut.

The swaying figure (SHAMBLER — a woman in a torn office coat) drags one foot past the bus, three metres away.
Its foot stops. It sniffs the air. Moves on.

[POV] Arin turns the phone over. Lock screen: **نیکا — ۱ ویدیوی جدید**. He stares. Under it: *ارسال: ۲ ساعت پیش*.

**آرین:** [whispers] [disbelief] نیکا؟...

### 1.2 — EXT. UNDER THE BUS / NIKA'S MESSAGE — NIGHT · 00:50–02:10
[POV, phone fills half the frame] Arin crawls out on the far side, crouches against the bus roof, turns the
brightness down and holds the phone to his ear on low volume.

THE VIDEO (handheld, white room, bad light): **NIKA** (16), loose black hair, hospital gown, a red-thread
bracelet on her wrist. She is filming herself, whispering, glancing at a door.

**نیکا (ویدیو):** [whispers] [shaky] آرین... اگه اینو می‌بینی، یعنی آقای مارک... [short pause] ولش کن. [swallows] من زنده‌م. همه‌ی این سال‌ها زنده بودم.

She turns her wrist to the camera: dark veins under the skin, a cannula taped there.

**نیکا (ویدیو):** [trembling] اونا می‌گن دارم خوب می‌شم. [voice breaks] دروغ می‌گن.

**نیکا (ویدیو):** [urgent] [whispers] مرکز... زیر تپه، آخر جاده‌ی معدن. [short pause] بیا دنبالم. خواهش می‌کنم.

A sound off-screen — a lock. Nika looks away, terrified, then back, very close to the lens:

**نیکا (ویدیو):** [whispers] [scared] به هیچ‌کس اعتماد نکن، حتی به —

The video cuts. Black. 00:41.

[3P, wide] Arin, small against the bus, the phone light on his face. He replays the last second. Behind him,
at the end of the street, a man in a plain bus-driver's shirt (the DRIVER — remember him) has stopped. He turns
his head toward the light.

**آرین:** [whispers] [to himself] [stunned] "همه‌ی این سال‌ها"... تو مُرده بودی.

### 1.3 — EXT. AVENUE — CONTINUOUS — THE ATTACK · 02:10–03:50
The DRIVER runs (RUNNER — fresh, fast). Arin hears it before he sees it: shoes slapping wet asphalt.

[POV] Arin bolts between cars. Mirrors, a car alarm he bumps starts and dies. Breathing, footsteps doubling his.
He vaults a car bonnet — [3P] the Driver goes over it on all fours.

[POV] A side street. A shopping trolley. Arin's foot catches a cable — he falls flat, phone skidding away.
[3P, low] He rolls onto his back — the Driver is on him, hands pinning his shoulders, face a hand-width away.
Grey skin, white eyes, mouth open.

[ECU] The Driver stops. It sniffs at Arin's neck, his cheek. A long breath in. It pulls its head back — almost
confused — makes a low sound in its chest, and climbs off him. It stands, looks around, and runs off after a
barking dog in the dark.

Silence. Arin lies there, shaking.

**آرین:** [whispers] [shaking] چرا... ولم کرد؟

He reaches for the phone — a hand grabs his ankle. A CRAWLER (a young man in a delivery jacket, legs broken)
under a parked car, pulling himself out fast. Arin kicks — the heel hits its face — he scrambles back, his left
palm comes down on a broken bottle.

**آرین:** [pained] [gasps] آخ —!

He gets up, grabs the phone, runs. [3P] The Crawler drags after him, slower and slower, and stops in the light
of a shop window, watching him go.

### 1.4 — INT. PHARMACY — NIGHT · 03:50–05:10
[POV] Arin shoulders a broken pharmacy door, pulls a shelf across it. Dark aisles. A TV on the wall, running on
a battery: a looping emergency card, white text on blue, no channel name:
*وضعیت اضطراری — منطقه تحت قرنطینه. اولین مورد تأییدشده: ۲۴ روز پیش، منطقه‌ی شمالی. افراد دارای تب، خود را معرفی کنند.*
(Northern district is where Arin lives — we see it again in 8.2.)

[3P, medium] He sits behind the counter, pours disinfectant over the cut palm, hisses, wraps it with gauze.
A flash — half a second, overexposed: a child's hand holding a smaller hand in a white corridor. A hum of a
lullaby. Gone. He rubs his eyes.

The phone rings. **Unknown number.** He lets it ring twice. Answers.

**صدای ناشناس (مارک):** [low] [rough] [whispers] آرین. پیامِ خواهرت رسید؟

**آرین:** [alarmed] تو کی هستی؟ نیکا کجاست؟

**صدای ناشناس:** [calm] [serious] قبل از طلوع آفتاب برس به مرکز. جاده‌ی قدیمی معدن، بعد از سد. [short pause] دم در فقط اسم کوچیکت رو بگو. هیچی بیشتر.

**آرین:** [angry] [shouting] جوابمو بده! تو کی هستی؟

**صدای ناشناس:** [quietly] [heavy] کسی که بهت بدهکاره.

Click. Arin calls back: *شماره در دسترس نیست.* Outside, something hits the pharmacy door. Again.

### 1.5 — EXT. CITY → MINE ROAD — NIGHT · 05:10–06:30
[POV] Out through the back of the pharmacy. A small car in the alley, keys in, a dead man's coat on the seat
(he pushes it out without looking). The engine starts on the third try.

Montage, all motivated by the drive (no empty shots longer than 4 s):
- [POV, windscreen] Empty boulevard. A roadblock — concrete blocks, a toppled barrier, no markings. He drives
  through the gap; a mirror snaps off.
- [3P, high] The car alone on a bridge; on the riverbank below, dozens of figures standing still in the fog,
  turning their heads together as the car passes.
- [POV] Radio static; between the hiss, a fragment: *«...دروازه‌ی قرنطینه... ساعت شش...»*
- [3P] The road leaves the city: the dam, then a gravel road up a dark hill, old mine conveyor rusting beside it.
- [POV] Headlights find a concrete portal cut into the hillside: a steel blast door, one camera, one intercom,

### 1.6 — EXT. FACILITY ENTRANCE — NIGHT · 06:30–07:30
[3P] Arin stands in the headlights, hand bandaged. He presses the intercom. A buzz. Nothing.
[CU] The camera above the door turns, slowly, and focuses on him.

Behind him, far down the gravel road: running footsteps. Many. [3P, wide] RUNNERS coming out of the fog,
up the hill, toward the light.

**آرین:** [panicked] [shouting] در رو باز کنید! خواهش می‌کنم!

He hits the door with his fist. The intercom clicks. A woman's voice — calm, precise (**ELENA**, unseen).

**صدای النا:** [calm] [cold] اسمت؟

[CU] Arin. The caller's words. The runners are fifty metres away.

**آرین:** [breathless] [hesitant] ...آرین.

A long pause on the intercom — too long. Then a deep mechanical clunk. The blast door rises a metre — red light
inside — and a rifle barrel comes out at Arin's face. Behind it, a man's eyes in the dark (**MARK**).

**مارک:** [hard] [loud] دستا بالا! [short pause] گاز گرفتنت؟ اگه گاز گرفتنت، همین‌جا تمومه.

CUT TO BLACK on the sound of the runners arriving. **END OF CHAPTER 1.**

# CHAPTER 2 — THE SAFE SHELTER · پناهگاه امن
**07:30 – 15:00 · 23:55–01:20 · airlock, reception, survivor room, med bay, corridor B1**
Objective: Arin gets inside, is refused Nika, learns the rules, finds an ally — and hears his sister.

### 2.1 — INT. AIRLOCK — NIGHT · 07:30–08:50
[3P] Mark drags Arin under the closing door. The door slams down on a runner's arm; the arm twitches, the door
crushes down, silence. Mark doesn't look at it.

A white steel airlock. UV light. Mark (52, grey stubble, plain dark security uniform, no badges) keeps the
rifle on him.

**مارک:** [commanding] کاپشن. پیرهن. بچرخ.

[3P] Arin strips to his T-shirt, turns, arms out. Mark checks his neck, arms. Stops at the bandaged palm.

**مارک:** [suspicious] این چیه؟

**آرین:** [defensive] شیشه‌ست. یه بطری شکسته.

Mark unwraps it roughly. A clean cut. A wall scanner sweeps Arin — a thermal outline — a soft tone: green,
*۳۶٫۸*. Mark lowers the rifle. Only then does he look at Arin's face — the light catches the small scar by the
eyebrow — and something passes over Mark's face. He looks away first.

**آرین:** [suspicious] تو بودی؟ پشت تلفن؟

**مارک:** [flat] نمی‌دونم از چی حرف می‌زنی. [short pause] لباساتو بپوش. دکتر منتظره.

### 2.2 — INT. RECEPTION — NIGHT · 08:50–10:10
Clean, cold fluorescent light; a long desk, old leather sofas, a wall of monitors showing the empty road
outside. A kettle. Too normal.

**DR. ELENA** (48, dark-blonde hair cut to the jaw, white lab coat over a grey turtleneck, a steel watch) pours
tea. She is warm, and she watches everything.

**النا:** [warm] [soft] شجاع بودی که تا اینجا اومدی. یا خوش‌شانس. [short pause] من النام. اینجا مسئولم.

**آرین:** [urgent] خواهرم اینجاست. نیکا. اون برام پیام فرستاده.

He holds out the phone, plays the video. [CU] Elena's eyes on the screen — on the bracelet. Nothing on her face.

**النا:** [gentle] [firm] آرین... اینجا پناهگاهه، نه بیمارستان. هیچ نیکایی اینجا نیست.

**آرین:** [angry] تو این ویدیو می‌گه مرکز. زیر تپه.

**النا:** [calm] [patient] اون اتاق شبیه هیچ جای اینجا نیست. [short pause] این روزها خیلی‌ها پیام‌های قدیمی می‌گیرن. شبکه‌ها خرابن.

**آرین:** [quietly] [hurt] اون دوازده سال پیش مُرد. من سر قبرش بودم.

Elena puts the cup in his good hand and holds it there one second too long.

**النا:** [softly] پس می‌دونی که نمی‌تونه اون باشه.

### 2.3 — INT. RECEPTION — CONTINUOUS — THE RULES · 10:10–10:50
Elena walks him toward the corridor, counting rules on her fingers — practical, kind, unbending.
[3P tracking, walk-and-talk — she never stops moving]

**النا:** [businesslike] چهار تا قانون. یک: هیچ‌کس پایین‌تر از طبقه‌ی B1 نمی‌ره. [short pause] دو: ساعت یک، خاموشی. سه: هر چهار ساعت، آزمایش خون. [short pause] چهار: درِ قرمز رو هیچ‌کس لمس نمی‌کنه.

**آرین:** [suspicious] چرا هر چهار ساعت؟

**النا:** [smiles] [cold] چون می‌خوایم زنده بمونید.

They pass the end of the corridor: a steel door painted dull red, a keycard reader, one red LED. Arin looks
back at it.

### 2.4 — INT. SURVIVOR ROOM — NIGHT · 10:50–12:10
A storage room turned dormitory: six bunks, a camping lamp, cans, a deck of cards. Two survivors.

**SARA** (23, dark brown hair in a low ponytail, olive field jacket, grey hoodie under it, black jeans, worn
boots) sits on a top bunk, knees up, watching the door, not Arin. **SAM** (32, heavy, red work fleece, cap,
cheerful and frightened) eats peaches from a can.

**سام:** [friendly] [nervous laugh] یه زنده‌ی دیگه! هلو می‌خوای؟ تاریخش گذشته ولی خوشمزه‌ست.

**سارا:** [dry] [suspicious] چطوری از جاده رد شدی؟ ما دیدیم چندتا بودن.

**آرین:** [hesitant] [quietly] یکی‌شون... روم بود. بعد ولم کرد.

Sara looks at him for the first time.

**سارا:** [cold] اونا ول نمی‌کنن.

She jumps down, goes to the door, runs her finger over the inside of the frame: scratch marks, and a steel bolt
on the *outside* she shows through the gap.

**سارا:** [low] [serious] شب‌ها درو از بیرون قفل می‌کنن. [short pause] از بیرون.

**سام:** [reassuring] [nervous] خب برای امنیتمونه دیگه. زامبی‌ها رو بیرون نگه می‌داره.

**سارا:** [sharp] زامبی‌ها بیرونِ کوهن، سام. [short pause] این قفل برای ماست.

Sara shows Arin a strip of grey duct tape folded in her palm, already cut to size. A small look between them —
the beginning of an alliance.

### 2.5 — INT. MED BAY — NIGHT · 12:10–13:10
A small white med bay. Elena takes Arin's blood herself; no nurse. Close shots: tourniquet, needle, the dark
tube filling.

[CU] Her hand trembles very slightly. She writes on the label — her thumb covers most of it; we glimpse **۰۰** —
and instead of putting the tube in the rack with SAM and SARA's tubes, she slips it into her coat pocket.

**آرین:** [casual] [watching] اونو نمی‌ذاری پیش بقیه؟

**النا:** [smooth] [warm] خونِ تو تازه از بیرون اومده. جدا آزمایشش می‌کنم. [short pause] احتیاط.

She tapes cotton on his arm and, without thinking, touches the scar by his eyebrow with her thumb, like a
mother. He pulls back. She takes her hand away.

**النا:** [quietly] [caught] ببخشید. [short pause] برو استراحت کن.

### 2.6 — INT. CORRIDOR B1 — NIGHT · 13:10–14:10
01:05. Lights dimmed to blue night mode. Arin goes to the washroom; on the way he finds Mark sitting on the
floor under a ventilation grille, smoking, the rifle across his knees.

**مارک:** [tired] [low] خاموشی ده دقیقه‌ی دیگه‌ست.

**آرین:** [quietly] اون صدا... پشت تلفن. خشن بود. مثل صدای تو.

**مارک:** [flat] نصف مردای این شهر صداشون خشنه. [short pause] [serious] اون فیلمی که به دکتر نشون دادی... به هیچ‌کس دیگه نشونش نده.

**آرین:** [pressing] چرا؟

Mark stubs out the cigarette, stands, and for a second looks like he will say it.

**مارک:** [softly] [heavy] برو بخواب، پسر.

### 2.7 — INT. SURVIVOR ROOM — NIGHT · 14:10–15:00
01:00. The lock clacks outside. Darkness. Sam snores. [3P, top shot] Arin on his bunk, eyes open.

Sound only, from the vent above the bunks: a faint metallic hum. Then — clearly — a girl humming a lullaby. The
same tune from the memory flash.

[CU] Arin sits up. [POV] The vent grille, black inside.

**نیکا (از دریچه):** [whispers] [distant] [echoing] ...آرین...

[CU] Arin's face. He turns — Sara, on the opposite bunk, is sitting up too, staring at the vent.

**سارا:** [whispers] [scared] تو هم شنیدی؟

CUT TO BLACK. **END OF CHAPTER 2.**
